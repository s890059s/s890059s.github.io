薪資計時 隱私權政策

開發者：Wei-Chieh Liu｜生效日期：2026 年 11 月 13 日

一句話說明

你的薪資、班表、休息紀錄和假期都只存在你的 iPhone 上。我們沒有伺服器、不需要帳號，也看不到你的任何資料。App 內的廣告由 Google AdMob 提供，AdMob 會依下方說明收集有限的裝置資訊。

存在你裝置上的資料

  • 月薪、幣別、工作時區、班表、發薪日
  • 喝水、休息等活動紀錄與自訂分類
  • 假期與休假日期、提醒設定

這些資料存在 App 自己的儲存空間，並使用 iOS 資料保護加密。我們不會上傳、出售或分享這些資料。刪除 App 就會刪除它們。

小工具

主畫面與鎖定畫面小工具透過 iOS 的 App Group 在你的裝置上讀取顯示所需的資料，例如金額和倒數。這些資料不會離開你的裝置。

行事曆（選用）

只有在你按下「從行事曆加入」時，App 才會請求行事曆權限，讀取今天起 12 個月內的全天事件，讓你挑選要加入的假期。App 只保存你選取的假期名稱與日期，不會寫入行事曆，也不會上傳。

通知（選用）

提醒是 iPhone 上的本機通知，只有在你啟用提醒時才請求權限。通知內容不包含薪資資料。

廣告（Google AdMob）

免費版會顯示 Google AdMob 廣告（開啟 App 時的全螢幕廣告，以及紀錄頁和休息頁底部的原生廣告）。為了提供廣告、計算成效與防止詐騙，Google 可能收集以下資訊：

  • 裝置識別碼（例如 IDFV，以及 Google 自己的廣告 Cookie 識別碼）
  • 由 IP 位址推算的粗略位置
  • 廣告互動資料，例如曝光與點擊
  • 當機與效能等診斷資訊

App 不會要求「App 追蹤透明度」授權，因此不會取得你的廣告識別碼（IDFA）。廣告 SDK 讀不到你的薪資、班表或紀錄。

如果你位於歐洲經濟區、英國或瑞士，App 會先透過 Google 的同意管理平台詢問你的同意。你隨時可以到「設定 › 隱私與小工具 › 廣告隱私選項」修改。

Google 如何使用資料：https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

兒童

Salary Timer Privacy Policy

Developer: Wei-Chieh Liu | Effective date: November 13, 2026

In short

Your salary, schedule, break records and days off stay on your iPhone. We run no servers, require no account and cannot see any of your data. Ads in the app are provided by Google AdMob, which collects limited device information as described below.

Data stored on your device

  • Monthly pay, currency, work time zone, schedule and payday
  • Break and activity records, and your custom categories
  • Holidays, leave days and reminder settings

This data is kept in the app's own storage and encrypted with iOS Data Protection. We do not upload, sell or share it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Widgets

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read the figures they display, such as amounts and countdowns, through an iOS App Group on your device. This data never leaves your device.

Calendar (optional)

The app asks for calendar access only when you tap "Add from Calendar". It reads all-day events for the next 12 months so you can pick holidays, and saves only the names and dates you choose. It never writes to your calendar or uploads anything.

Notifications (optional)

Reminders are local notifications on your iPhone. Permission is requested only when you turn a reminder on, and notifications never include salary data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows Google AdMob ads: a full-screen ad when the app opens, and native ads at the bottom of the History and Breaks screens. To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the IDFV and Google's own advertising identifiers
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interaction data, such as impressions and clicks
  • Diagnostics, such as crash and performance data

The app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, so it never accesses your Advertising Identifier (IDFA). The ad SDK cannot read your salary, schedule or records.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent management platform first. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy & widgets › Ad privacy options.

How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
