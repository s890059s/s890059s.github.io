常見問題

我的薪資資料會上傳嗎？
不會。所有資料只存在你的 iPhone，不需要帳號。刪除 App 就會刪除資料。
顯示的金額和我實際領到的不一樣？
金額是依你輸入的月薪和班表平均分攤的估算，不包含稅、勞健保或加班費。
怎麼加入國定假日？
到「設定 › 假期與休假」按「從行事曆加入」，選擇要加入的假日。也可以手動新增特休或補假。
小工具怎麼加？
在主畫面長按空白處，點左上角的「＋」或「編輯」，搜尋「薪資計時」。鎖定畫面小工具則是長按鎖定畫面後選「自訂」。
怎麼修改廣告同意設定？
位於歐洲經濟區、英國或瑞士的使用者，可以到「設定 › 隱私與小工具 › 廣告隱私選項」修改。

FAQ

Is my salary data uploaded?
No. All data stays on your iPhone and no account is needed. Deleting the app deletes the data.
Why doesn't the amount match my actual pay?
Amounts are estimates spread evenly over the pay and schedule you enter. They don't include tax, insurance or overtime.
How do I add public holidays?
Go to Settings › Holidays & time off and tap "Add from Calendar", then choose the holidays to add. You can also add leave days yourself.
How do I add a widget?
Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, tap "+" or "Edit", and search for Salary Timer. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen and tap "Customize".
How do I change my ad consent?
Users in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland can change it in Settings › Privacy & widgets › Ad privacy options.
