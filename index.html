Barcode Pets

Developer: Wei-Chieh Liu

An offline game in which you raise companions by scanning product barcodes. Scan EAN/UPC barcodes around you to hatch, raise and evolve companions; your daily steps also earn in-game rewards. No account needed and game data stays on your phone. The game offers optional rewarded ads served by Google AdMob.
